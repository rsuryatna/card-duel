EMBER & TIDE
An elemental card duel — outwit the Tidecaller
Fire Water Earth Air
Fire beats Air · Air beats Earth · Earth beats Water · Water beats Fire — advantage deals +2 damage
How to play: each turn you gain energy and draw a card. Click a card in your hand to play it onto your side of the table (up to 3 cards). When you press End Turn, your cards attack — left to right, into the card across from them, or straight at the Tidecaller. Reduce the Tidecaller to 0 life to win. Keyboard: E ends the turn, Space plays the selected card, 1–7 select a card.
